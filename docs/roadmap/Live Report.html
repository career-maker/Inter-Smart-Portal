Intersmart Portal: Internal Software Plan

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Tests v1.2

One connected workflow from the first sales lead to a delivered, paid and reviewed project, built on the existing Employee Portal without disturbing it.

The journey of one project

Each step hands the project to the next team automatically and tells them.

  1. Sales

    Live

    Records leads and follow-ups, then creates the project with platform, pages, quality level and budget. The project is linked to the salesperson.

  2. Accounts

    Live

    Is told a new project awaits its advance. Approves or rejects payments, raises the budget, holds or terminates, sets sales targets and keeps the AMC list. Sales is told when payment is approved.

  3. Project Office (PMO)

    Live

    Assigns a coordinator, gives each department its budget, holds a project with a reason, approves out-of-scope work. Work tabs stay locked until the advance is approved.

  4. Project Coordinator

    Live

    Accepts the project, fills the business analysis, adds client resources (logo, domain, brochure) and asks for out-of-scope work with effort.

  5. Design (UI) team

    Live

    Defines pages and sections, works pages through the status chain (revisions, feedback, corrections), all inside the design budget.

  6. UX and Dev teams

    Building

    Team lead plans sprints with effort, the project manager approves against the budget, the coordinator requests dates, resources report daily progress.

  7. QA team

    Building

    Finished work moves to QA automatically. QA lists bugs, the team lead verifies, and overruns alert the project manager.

  8. Go live and after-sales

    Planned

    System admin allocates the server and marks the project live. AMC renewals are tracked; customer feedback and appreciation follow.

  9. HR and Management

    Planned

    Performance by department and person, leave approvals (already live), ratings and dashboards for every role.

How we keep it safe

  • Nothing is released without a database backup, a preview of the change and a check of the live site afterwards.
  • Each new module has an on/off switch and starts hidden. It is switched on only when it is ready.
  • Access follows the department: the Sales team sees Sales, the Accounts team sees Accounts, and so on. Nobody sees more than their job needs.
  • Existing modules (leave, attendance, projects, TA, chat) were not changed and keep working.
  • Every action that matters leaves a trail: who did it, when and why (audit log and history).

Can the system handle 100+ users at once?

The database will not break with 100+ users working at once. The live server is strong (24 processors, 40 GB memory). Improvements planned in Phase 0 (lighter background checks, rate limits, caching) give comfortable room for 150 to 300 users at the same time. A load test will confirm the exact number.
