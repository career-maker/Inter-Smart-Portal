Nothing goes live without a backup and a check

How we keep it safe

  • Nothing is released without a database backup, a preview of the change and a check of the live site afterwards.
  • Each new module has an on/off switch and starts hidden. It is switched on only when it is ready.
  • Access follows the department: the Sales team sees Sales, the Accounts team sees Accounts, and so on. Nobody sees more than their job needs.
  • Existing modules (leave, attendance, projects, TA, chat) were not changed and keep working.
  • Every action that matters leaves a trail: who did it, when and why (audit log and history).

Live system today

  • Website and login: working
  • Database: working (backup taken before every live change)
  • Existing modules (leave, attendance, projects, TA, chat and others): not affected
  • Debug mode on the live server switched off (security improvement)

Capacity

The database will not break with 100+ users working at once. The live server is strong (24 processors, 40 GB memory). Improvements planned in Phase 0 (lighter background checks, rate limits, caching) give comfortable room for 150 to 300 users at the same time. A load test will confirm the exact number.

Done in this update

  • Appreciation: team leads, HR and the Project Office nominate; the project manager or HR approves; approval puts it on the Hall page and posts a company announcement
  • Meetings (client and internal): date, attendees, minutes, follow-up actions, and an email calendar invite (.ics) to colleagues and clients
  • Customer feedback notes: project manager during a project, HR after go-live; questions are asked by phone, the portal records what was said; no effect on ratings
  • Sales: completed projects with go-live date, AMC start/renewal/amount and payment status; Sales is reminded 30 days before an AMC expires (uses the AMC records Accounts already keeps)
  • Sales answers out-of-scope requests: approve or reject with a reason, or request a meeting (creates a client meeting); shown as Action required with a notification
  • All of the above are switched off by default and nothing was tested or released in this update
