This guide walks the portal module by module, in the order you'd naturally demo it: log in, manage people, run leave and attendance, handle travel allowance, then the supporting modules. Each entry names who can use it, where to click, and the exact steps.

Three roles exist — Super Admin (full control, cannot apply leave for themself), Team Lead (own team + own requests), Employee (own records only). The matrix below is the fast reference; the sections after it are the walkthrough.

Getting started

1.Login

Super AdminTeam LeadEmployee

Public page, before authentication · /login

Email + password sign-in via Laravel Sanctum. Landing page after login depends on role.

  1. Open the portal URL — the login screen loads.
  2. Enter your work email and password.
  3. Click Log In.
  4. You land on your role's Dashboard, and the side menu shows only the modules your role can use.
Forgot password: click "Forgot Password" on the login screen, enter your email, follow the reset link sent to it, then set a new password.
Screenshot slot — Login screen

2.Dashboard

Super AdminTeam LeadEmployee

Home page after login · Dashboard in the side menu

Role-specific landing view — the content shown changes per role, the module itself doesn't.

  1. Super Admin sees company-wide KPI cards: headcount, pending leave/TA approvals, today's attendance.
  2. Team Lead sees their own welcome card plus a snapshot of their team and pending approvals.
  3. Employee sees a welcome card ("You have been with Intersmart for X Years Y Months Z Days", calculated from joining date), their own attendance widget, announcements, and birthdays.
  4. Use the quick links on the cards to jump straight into a module.

People & teams

3.Employee management

Super AdminTeam Lead (view own team)

Employees in the side menu

Full employee records: personal, contact, employment info, status, and photo.

  1. Employees → Add Employee.
  2. Fill in personal details (name, DOB, gender, blood group, marital status), contact & address, and employment info (designation, team/department, joining date).
  3. Assign a team from the dynamic department list (pulled live from Team management — no hardcoded options).
  4. Save — the system assigns a unique employee code and the record appears in the list.
  5. To edit: open the employee → update fields → Save. To change status: set it to Active / Disabled / Resigned / Terminated.
  6. To add a photo: open the employee → Upload Photo → choose an image → Save.
Screenshot slot — Employee list & Add/Edit form

4.Team / department management

Super Admin

Teams in the side menu

Create departments and assign each one a Team Lead.

  1. Teams → Add Team.
  2. Enter the team name and description, and pick a Team Lead from the employee list.
  3. Save — a unique team code is generated automatically from the name (e.g. "Quality Assurance" → QUAL-3847).
  4. The new team is immediately available in the Employee form's department dropdown.
Screenshot slot — Team list & Add Team form

Leave management

5.Apply for leave

Team LeadEmployee

Leaves → Apply Leave (this item is hidden from Super Admin's menu — Super Admin cannot apply leave for themself)

Submit a leave request against Casual or Sick Leave (or a half-day/WFH variant).

  1. Leaves → Apply Leave.
  2. Choose the leave type.
  3. Pick start and end date, and enter a reason.
  4. Submit — the request enters a two-stage approval: Team Lead status, then Admin status.
Rule: Casual Leave must be applied at least 3 days in advance (enforced by the backend). Sick Leave has no advance-notice rule.
Screenshot slot — Apply Leave form

6.Approve / reject leave

Team Lead (own team, 1st stage)Super Admin (final stage)

Leaves in the side menu, pending-requests list

Two-stage sign-off: Team Lead approves first, Super Admin approves final.

  1. Leaves → open a pending request.
  2. Review the dates, reason, and the employee's remaining balance.
  3. Team Lead: Approve or Reject (first stage).
  4. Once Team Lead-approved, it appears for Super Admin's final Approve/Reject.
  5. Super Admin can also override a prior decision if a mistake needs correcting.
Screenshot slot — Leave approval screen

7.View leave balance

Super Admin (everyone)Team Lead (own)Employee (own)

Own balance widget on Dashboard/Profile for Team Lead & Employee · dedicated Leave Balances page for Super Admin

Shows Casual Leave balance, CL carry-forward (with its expiry year), Sick Leave balance, and total leave taken.

  1. Team Lead / Employee: check the balance widget on the Dashboard or Profile page.
  2. Super Admin: open Leave Balances to see every employee's figures in one table.
Screenshot slot — Leave balance view

8.Leave balance management

Super Admin

Leave Balances in the side menu (Super Admin only)

Manually adjust any employee's leave figures, with every change logged.

  1. Leave Balances → search or select an employee.
  2. Review their current Casual Leave, CL carry-forward, Sick Leave, and total taken.
  3. Click Adjust, enter the new value and a remark explaining the change.
  4. Save — the change is written to the Leave Balance Audit Log (previous value, new value, who made the change, remark).
Carry-forward rule: 12 CL allocated every Jan 1; unused CL carries forward for exactly one year, then expires Dec 31 of that following year; carry-forward balance is always consumed before the current year's allocation.
Sick Leave rule: 12 SL allocated every Jan 1, no carry-forward — unused SL simply expires Dec 31.
Screenshot slot — Leave Balance Management + Audit Log

9.Annual leave allocation

Super Admin

Settings → Run Annual Allocation (manual) · runs automatically every Jan 1

Grants the new year's 12 CL + 12 SL to every employee and rolls unused CL into carry-forward.

  1. Automatic: a scheduled job runs at midnight on Jan 1 and applies the allocation to every employee — no action needed.
  2. Manual (if it needs re-running, e.g. after a missed cron): Settings → Run Annual Allocation → confirm.
Screenshot slot — Run Annual Allocation control

10.Attendance check-in / check-out

Super Admin (views all)Team LeadEmployee

Attendance in the side menu

Daily check-in/out with optional break tracking.

  1. Attendance → Check In — timestamp is recorded.
  2. Optionally start/end a break during the day.
  3. Check Out at end of day.
  4. The history list shows date, check-in/out times, breaks, and computed status per day.

11.Work from home (WFH) requests

Employee (apply)Team Lead (apply + approve)Super Admin (approve)

WFH in the side menu

Request-and-approve flow for remote-work days, same pattern as leave.

  1. WFH → New Request.
  2. Pick the date(s) and a reason.
  3. Submit — it routes to the approver.
  4. Approver opens WFH → reviews → Approve or Reject.

Travel allowance (TA)

12.Apply for travel allowance

Team LeadEmployee

TA → Apply (not shown to Super Admin — TA management covers that role instead)

Claim travel expenses with an itemised breakdown and bill upload.

  1. TA → Apply.
  2. Enter the reason for travel and the date travelled.
  3. Add expense line items — category (Travel / Food / Accommodation / Other), amount, description — click Add Item for each one.
  4. Upload the bill/receipt.
  5. Submit — the total is calculated automatically from the line items and the request starts as Applied.
Heads up for the demo: applying is meant to email HR@intersmart.in and Ameesha@intersmart.in with one-click Approve/Reject links, but SMTP isn't configured in this environment yet — that email won't actually land. See Coming soon.
Screenshot slot — TA Apply form

13.TA status (my requests)

Team Lead (own)Employee (own)

TA → Status

Track your own travel-allowance requests through their lifecycle.

  1. TA → Status.
  2. Filter by status: Applied / Approved / Paid / Unpaid / Rejected.
  3. Open a request to see its expense breakdown, any approver notes, and payment status.
Screenshot slot — TA Status list

14.TA management

Super Admin

TA → Management (Super Admin only)

Review, approve, reject, and mark travel claims as paid.

  1. TA → Management — lists every employee's requests, filterable by status.
  2. Open a request → review the expense breakdown and uploaded bill.
  3. Approve (optionally add notes) or Reject (with a reason).
  4. Once actually paid out, toggle Mark as Paid for payroll tracking.
HR can also approve/reject straight from the alert email's signed link (no login needed) — it drops them into this page with the request pre-expanded, once email sending is live.

Your own personal, contact, and employment details.

23.Profile update requests

Team Lead (submit)Employee (submit)Super Admin (approve)

Submitted from Profile · reviewed under Profile Requests (Super Admin only)

Keeps sensitive personal-data edits auditable instead of silent.

  1. Team Lead / Employee: edit a sensitive field on Profile and submit — this creates a pending request rather than saving directly.
  2. Super Admin: Profile Requests → open a pending change → compare old vs. requested value.
  3. Approve (applies the change) or Reject.
